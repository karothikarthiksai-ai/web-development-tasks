NO SHORTCUTS ON SAFETY

Adventure is better
when you return.

The mountains deserve respect. Make safety part of your plan before you take the first step.

01

Know Your Limits

Select routes that match your fitness and experience. Acclimatize when required.

02

Check the Weather

Review local forecasts, warnings and trail conditions before departure.

03

Stay With Your Group

Follow your guide, communicate regularly and never wander off alone.

04

Protect the Wild

Carry out waste, respect wildlife and follow local camping and fire rules.

!

Before you leave: Share your itinerary and expected return time with a trusted person. Carry appropriate emergency supplies and know the local rescue procedure.
